# Founding Principles

Status: Draft

## Principle 1

The repository is the project record. Humans are the owners. Knowledge Agents are collaborators.

## Principle 2

If a design decision matters, it belongs in the repository.

## Principle 3

Conversations generate ideas. Repositories preserve decisions.

## Principle 4

Specifications describe concepts. Implementations describe mechanisms.

## Principle 5

Knowledge is not captured. Information is captured. Knowledge is refined.

## Principle 6

The specification must outlive its implementations.

## Principle 7

Evidence and understanding are different.

## Principle 8

History is preserved. Knowledge evolves without erasing its past.

## Principle 9

Design decisions should pass the Twenty-Year Test.

Ask: if this project succeeds, will this decision still make sense twenty years from now?

## Principle 10

No insight is too small to capture. No insight is important enough to skip refinement.
