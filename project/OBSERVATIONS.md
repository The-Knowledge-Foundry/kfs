# Observations

Observations record what has been noticed while building Knowledge Foundry.

They are not decisions.

## O-0001

Architectural ideas emerged faster than they could be incorporated into permanent documentation.

## O-0002

Conversation alone proved insufficient for preserving architectural continuity.

## O-0003

Separating active reasoning from accepted knowledge reduced uncertainty about where ideas belonged.

## O-0004

The process of designing Knowledge Foundry generated evidence that informed the architecture of the system.

## O-0005

The need for continuity arose before any formal specification existed.
