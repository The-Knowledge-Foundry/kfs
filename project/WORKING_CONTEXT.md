## Current Understanding

Knowledge Foundry is an open specification effort for engineering durable knowledge systems.

The project is not an Obsidian vault, a note-taking workflow, a database schema, or an AI tool.

## North Star

Knowledge Foundry is an open specification for engineering durable knowledge systems whose conclusions remain explainable, whose evidence remains traceable, and whose knowledge evolves without losing its history.

## Founding Working Insight

Knowledge is not captured. Information is captured. Knowledge is refined.
