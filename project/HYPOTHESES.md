# Hypotheses

Hypotheses are experimental ideas that may later become design decisions.

They are not part of the specification.

## H-0001

Knowledge is not captured. Information is captured. Knowledge is refined.

Status: Experimental

## H-0002

Knowledge systems benefit from explicitly separating active reasoning from accepted knowledge.

Status: Experimental

## H-0003

Working Contexts improve continuity between contributors and Knowledge Agents.

Status: Experimental

## H-0004

Knowledge has identifiable stages of maturity.

Status: Experimental

## H-0005

The process used to create a knowledge system can provide evidence for the design of that system.

Status: Experimental
