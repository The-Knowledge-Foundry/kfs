# KFD-0000: Why Knowledge Foundry Exists

Status: Draft  
Author: Kory Ronan  
Sprint: Sprint 0 — Foundation

## Summary

Knowledge Foundry exists to define an open specification for engineering durable knowledge systems.

## Problem

Human knowledge is one of the most valuable assets individuals and organizations possess, yet most knowledge systems treat it as an accidental by-product of work rather than an asset to be engineered, maintained, and preserved.

Information is scattered across documents, conversations, files, emails, systems, and memory. Over time, it becomes fragmented, stale, untrusted, or lost.

## Core Belief

Knowledge is not captured. Information is captured. Knowledge is refined.

## Mission

Knowledge Foundry develops open specifications for engineering durable, evidence-based knowledge systems.

## Non-Goals

Knowledge Foundry is not:

- A note-taking app
- An Obsidian vault
- A database schema
- A prompt library
- A single AI workflow
- A task management system
